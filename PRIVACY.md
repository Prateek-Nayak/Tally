# Privacy policy (draft)

> This is a starting template for the team to iterate on, not legal advice.
> Tally handles other people's financial data — have this reviewed by
> someone qualified before it's shown to a real user, and before launch in
> any jurisdiction with specific requirements (GDPR, India's DPDP Act,
> etc.).

## What we collect

- **Account data**: name and email address, used for sign-in and to
  identify you to people in your groups.
- **Financial data you enter**: expenses, amounts, group membership, and
  settlements you or people in your groups record. This is shared with
  other members of the same group by design — that's how the app works.
- **Ghost members**: a name you enter for someone who hasn't signed up.
  We store only what you provide; that person is not otherwise notified or
  tracked until they create an account and a link is confirmed.

## What we don't do

- We don't sell your data.
- We don't show ads.
- We don't share your data with anyone outside your groups except where
  required by law.

## Retention

- Deleted expenses, settlements, and group memberships are kept in a
  recoverable Trash for 30 days, then permanently removed.
- Audit log entries (who changed what, when) are retained separately for
  accountability and dispute resolution within a group; retention period
  to be finalized before launch.

## Your rights

- You can request a copy of your data.
- You can request account deletion; this removes your profile and
  anonymizes your name on historical records that other group members
  still rely on for shared expense history.
